# Gate-by-gate review rubric

## Gate 1 — Theory

Pass when the mechanism is named, boundary conditions are stated, rival explanations are addressed, and the design can falsify the mechanism.

Conditional when the theory is plausible but the mechanism is not operationalized or rivals are unaddressed.

Fail when the theory is keyword-matched, or the design cannot distinguish the theory from a rival.

## Gate 2 — Construct

Pass when every construct has a canonical name, stable definition, and documented discriminant boundary.

Conditional when synonyms are unresolved or names shift slightly between sections.

Fail when constructs are invented ad hoc, merged across sections, or changed to fit results.

## Gate 3 — Measurement

Pass when instruments are validated scales or audited operationalizations with reliability and validity evidence appropriate to the population.

Conditional when translation/adaptation lacks invariance evidence or item modifications reuse original validity.

Fail when alpha-only evidence is used for a claim that requires structural validity, or an unvalidated ad hoc measure is treated as validated.

## Gate 4 — Design / identification

Pass when the design supports the causal or descriptive language used.

Conditional when the design is adequate but exclusions, attrition, or confound checks are missing.

Fail when causal language exceeds the design (cross-sectional causality, unmanipulated label experiments claimed as institutional effects, prediction presented as causal).

## Gate 5 — Novelty

Pass when the contribution is new relative to the theoretical or empirical literature, not just the dataset or setting.

Conditional when novelty is asserted but prior work is not distinguished.

Fail when the paper repeats an established effect in a new convenience sample with no boundary revision.

## Gate 6 — Contribution

Pass when the paper would change a field-level inference or measurement practice.

Conditional when the contribution is local and needs a boundary argument.

Fail when the discussion claims field-level change the design cannot support.

## Gate 7 — Evidence-to-claim

Pass when every claim is supported by the reported number, reliability, diagnostic, or verified source.

Conditional when support exists but effect sizes, uncertainty, or exclusions are underreported.

Fail when a claim outruns the evidence (e.g., frequency interpreted as meaning change, classifier accuracy interpreted as public opinion).

## Output format

```text
| Gate | Verdict | Severity | Location | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```

Follow with the three priority revision actions and the not-examined list.
