# Section-sensitive prose editing

## Abstract

Remove generic openings and method inventories. State the communication problem, exact
gap, design/material, principal result or proposed test, contribution, and boundary.
Do not add unexplained abbreviations, citations unless the venue expects them, or claims
that appear nowhere in the manuscript.

## Introduction

Favor problem and puzzle over broad scene-setting. Compress literature catalogs into a
tension or missing inference. State the contribution before the reader must infer it.
Do not exaggerate novelty to compensate for a weak gap.

## Literature and theory

Replace author-by-author summaries with comparison and synthesis. Name the mechanism,
construct relation, scope condition, and rival explanation. Repetition of a construct
name is often necessary; do not synonymize it away.

## Methods

Clarity and reproducibility outrank stylistic variety. Passive voice is acceptable when
the procedure matters more than the actor; active voice is preferable when responsibility
for a decision matters. Do not delete apparent repetition if each occurrence fixes a
different sample, model, instrument, or analysis population.

## Results

Lead with the tested quantity and result. Keep estimates, uncertainty, denominators,
units, model/table locators, nulls, and diagnostics intact. Remove evaluative adjectives,
causal interpretation not licensed by design, and repeated table narration.

## Discussion

Make the first paragraph answer the RQ/H. Organize later paragraphs around theoretical
change, rivals, boundary conditions, communication implications, limitations, and next
tests. Replace “important implications” with the exact inference or practice affected.

## Limitations

Avoid ritual self-criticism and exhaustive apology. For each limitation state the source,
the claim it weakens, its likely direction when knowable, and the design or evidence that
would address it. Do not minimize a limitation that invalidates the central inference.

## Conclusion

Remove generic restatement and future-work padding. State what was learned, what changed
for communication theory or method, and where the claim stops.

## Chinese and English

- Chinese: reduce slogan-like parallelism, “随着……发展,” empty “具有重要意义,” and
  stacked four-character evaluation; preserve explicit logical relations.
- English: reduce generic landscape openings, excessive nominalization, uniform
  transitions, and stacked hedging; preserve calibrated modality and terminology.
