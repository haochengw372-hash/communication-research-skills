# Reducing defensive academic writing

## What defensive writing is

Defensive writing protects the author from imagined criticism by making the proposition
hard to locate or evaluate. It is different from justified caution, which exposes the
exact inferential boundary.

| Defensive pattern | Why it fails | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| “It could perhaps be argued that X may…” | distributes responsibility across hedges | state who argues X and retain one evidence-calibrated modal |
| “Despite several limitations, the findings nevertheless…” | apologizes before naming the finding | state the finding, then name the limitation that changes its scope |
| “It is beyond the scope…” repeated across sections | accumulates nonclaims instead of defining scope | give one scope paragraph and only repeat when interpretation changes |
| “Prior studies have extensively demonstrated…” | invokes anonymous consensus | name the supported consensus and the relevant disagreement |
| “These findings provide valuable implications…” | avoids committing to the implication | state what theoretical expectation, boundary, or practice changes |
| Long citation cluster after a broad sentence | uses references as armor | synthesize agreement, disagreement, method, or boundary among sources |

## Hedge calibration

Choose the verb from the evidence type:

- descriptive evidence: `documents`, `shows in this corpus`, `is associated with`;
- predictive evidence: `predicts in held-out data`, `improves calibration`;
- experimental evidence: `affected` for the randomized treatment contrast within the
  design boundary;
- quasi-experimental evidence: causal language only with the estimand and identifying
  assumptions visible;
- qualitative evidence: `participants described`, `the analysis interprets`, `the case
  indicates` within the case and sampling boundary;
- simulation: `is sufficient to generate`, `reproduces under tested conditions`;
- theory: `proposes`, `predicts`, `is consistent with`, or `does not distinguish`.

One accurate boundary is stronger than several generic hedges.

## Claim-first revision

Move the controlling proposition into the first or second sentence of a paragraph.
Follow with the evidence, mechanism or synthesis that earns it. Put a limitation where
it changes interpretation, not automatically at the beginning or end of every paragraph.

## Preserve legitimate caution

Keep uncertainty intervals, rival explanations, measurement error, sample restrictions,
identification assumptions, negative cases, and unresolved evidence. Specific caution
is part of the result; removing it is not confident writing but scientific distortion.
