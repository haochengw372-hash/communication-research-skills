## Conditions

| Condition | What varies | What is held constant | Construct intended |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

Rules:

- Each row varies one intended construct.
- Holding constant is explicit: text length, visual layout, author identity, platform branding, order, and response format.

## Randomization and sample

- Randomization unit and procedure.
- Strata if used.
- Target effect and power (n per cell, expected difference or interaction).
- Inclusion/exclusion and attention-check rules written before fielding.

## Manipulation checks

- Item(s), expected direction, threshold, timing (before outcomes).
- Falsification item if needed (e.g., participants should NOT infer a different construct).
- Pre-registered decision: what happens if a cell fails the check (retain all, sensitivity, exclude pre-specified).

## Analysis plan

- Primary model with all terms.
- Interaction decomposition.
- Robustness and sensitivity analyses.
- Missing-data and attrition rules.

## Preregistration checklist

- [ ] Hypotheses with direction.
- [ ] Conditions and randomization.
- [ ] Manipulation checks and thresholds.
- [ ] Primary and secondary outcomes.
- [ ] Exclusion rules.
- [ ] Analysis plan with model formula.
- [ ] Sample-size rule and stopping rule.
- [ ] Ethics and debriefing plan.

## Communication-specific audit

- [ ] AI/disclosure cue not bundled with other cues.
- [ ] State/commercial or platform cue pretested as intended.
- [ ] Perceived humanness and machine agency measured separately when both are plausible.
- [ ] Platform materials version recorded and dated.
- [ ] No claim stronger than the design (label-only study = labeling question only).
