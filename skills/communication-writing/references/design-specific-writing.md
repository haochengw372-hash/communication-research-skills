# Design-specific writing for communication research

## Survey and measurement studies

Report sampling frame, recruitment, nonresponse/weights, construct definitions, item
source and adaptation, response format, missingness, dimensionality, reliability beyond
alpha alone, CFA/IRT, validity, and invariance before group comparisons. Do not describe
cross-sectional associations as effects or mechanisms.

## Experiments

Report randomization unit, allocation, conditions, held-constant stimulus features,
pretest/manipulation evidence, attention checks, exclusions, attrition, power/stopping,
primary contrasts, multiplicity, and intention-to-treat results. A measured mediator is
not automatically a causally identified mechanism.

## Computational content analysis

Report corpus population and sampling, collection dates/method, unitization,
deduplication, language, codebook, annotation independence, reliability sample,
dictionary/model/prompt versions, human validation, leakage, robustness, ethics, and
label-to-claim boundary. Topic prevalence is not public opinion or causal influence.

## Network studies

Report node/edge meanings, graph boundary, direction/weight/time, missing ties, bots,
components, projection/aggregation, model terms, convergence/fit, and sensitivity.
Centrality is not influence; similarity is not contagion without a design.

## Causal and policy studies

Lead with treatment, counterfactual, estimand, timing, assignment/identifying variation,
population, and assumptions. Pair every assumption with diagnostics or falsification.
Report overlap, pre-trends, placebos, bandwidth/donor/instrument sensitivity, spillovers,
and treatment versions as applicable.

## Temporal, spatial, and multimodal studies

- Temporal: clock, interval, lag, risk set, censoring, attrition, autocorrelation,
  seasonality, aggregation, and timing boundary.
- Spatial: unit, geocoding match/uncertainty, CRS, joins, weights, MAUP, privacy,
  ecological boundary, and map uncertainty.
- Multimodal: media sampling, frame/scene/speaker units, OCR/ASR or detector error,
  model/version, human reference, cross-modal relation, bias, copyright, and modality
  loss.

## Simulation studies

Separate code verification, micro validation, macro calibration, and held-out
validation. Report rules, topology, schedule, parameters, initial conditions, seeds,
uncertainty, sensitivity, equifinality, and rival models. Prefer “sufficient to generate
the pattern under tested conditions” over claims that simulated agents prove human
behavior.

## Qualitative studies

Report case and participant selection, access, consent, material generation, researcher
position, reflexivity, coding/interpretive procedure, information power or saturation
logic, negative cases, and evidence locators. Quotes exemplify an interpretation; they
do not replace the analytic account.

## Mixed methods

State design type, strand priority and sequence, connecting sample or instrument,
integration point, joint display, and rules for convergent, complementary, and discordant
evidence. Do not force the strands to agree or let one strand silently answer the other's
question.

## Review and conceptual papers

Reviews state question, sources, queries, dates, screening, synthesis logic, coverage,
and evidence limits. Conceptual papers reconstruct a problem in existing vocabulary,
define the proposed mechanism or framework, distinguish neighbors, apply it to evidence
or cases, and state what observation could challenge it. Neither genre is an invitation
to cite broadly without claim-level support.
