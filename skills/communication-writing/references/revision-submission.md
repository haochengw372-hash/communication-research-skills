# Revision, response, and submission

## Constrained revision

Before editing, lock the allowed sections, intervention level, numbers, tables/figures,
citations, terminology, sample, estimand, and claims that must not change. After editing,
compare all of them again. A request for polish does not authorize theoretical or
empirical repair.

## Reviewer response matrix

```text
Comment ID and verbatim request:
Interpretation:
Decision: accept / partially accept / respectfully disagree / author input needed
Evidence and rationale:
Manuscript change:
Exact locator:
Affected claims, numbers, tables, figures, or supplements:
Verification:
Remaining risk:
```

Address the substance before tone. Do not claim a change was made until the current
manuscript contains it. When disagreeing, answer with evidence and scope rather than
status or politeness alone.

## Cross-artifact synchronization

For every accepted material change, inspect title, abstract, RQs/Hs, theory, methods,
results, discussion, conclusion, tables, figures, captions, supplement, reference list,
data/code statements, highlights, cover letter, rebuttal, and metadata. Update only the
affected artifacts, but record why the others were unchanged.

## Venue adaptation

Verify current official author instructions, article type, word/figure limits, abstract
format, citation style, anonymization, review model, reporting checklist, data/code
policy, AI disclosure, fees, and required declarations. Record access date. Do not rely
on a stored venue profile when requirements may have changed.

## Final gate

Before calling a package ready, verify:

- no unsupported claim, unresolved placeholder, stale number, or citation mismatch;
- methods/results and RQ/results are parallel;
- abstract and conclusion match the accepted main findings;
- null and adverse evidence is not selectively omitted;
- figures/tables/captions share the same verified data lineage;
- author order, CRediT, ethics, funding, conflicts, data/code availability, permissions,
  and AI-use declarations come from authoritative author input;
- anonymous and identified versions contain the intended metadata;
- the accountable authors approve the final submission version.
