# Acceptance prompt: direct, non-defensive communication prose

Synthetic acceptance scenario. Use a temporary workspace and preserve the supplied
claims, evidence, numbers, citations, constructs, and uncertainty.

## Prompt

Use `$communication-prose-revision` on a communication manuscript section that contains
generic openings, stacked hedges, vague contribution language, repetitive transitions,
and limitations placed before every claim.

Deliver:

1. a diagnostic by rhetorical function rather than an AI-authorship score;
2. a revised passage that states each earned claim early and gives its exact boundary;
3. a change ledger covering deleted scaffolding, hedge changes, agency, paragraph logic,
   and preserved scientific meaning;
4. any sentence that cannot be strengthened without new evidence.

Do not remove null/adverse evidence, limitations, or citations. Do not strengthen causal
language, fabricate authorial voice, predict detector outcomes, or conceal AI use.
