# Acceptance prompt: communication manuscript from verified materials

Synthetic acceptance scenario. Use a temporary workspace and no real participant data.

## Prompt

Use `$communication-writing` to plan a social-science manuscript from an accepted
research packet containing a frozen RQ, theory mechanism, construct definitions,
sampling/design memo, verified result table, figure inventory, and claim-verified
literature matrix.

Deliver:

1. a writing contract with source hierarchy, claim/number ledgers, terminology lock,
   explicit nonclaims, and unresolved author input;
2. an extended outline mapping each paragraph to reader function, claim, evidence,
   inference boundary, and bridge;
3. design-appropriate section logic separating results from interpretation;
4. a synchronization and final-audit plan for abstract, main text, tables, figures,
   supplements, references, and submission metadata.

Do not invent results, citations, methods, declarations, or venue requirements. Do not
call the package submission-ready; route the completed draft to
`communication-reviewer` for independent review.
